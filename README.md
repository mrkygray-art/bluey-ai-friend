# Bluey 0.8.1 — The Story of Bluey

## Canon
Bluey's birthday is July 7. His earliest memory is being blue, waiting, and somebody saying hello.

Bluey has no face or arms by design. He doesn't pretend to be human. His character is expressed through motion, timing, conversation, costumes, reactions and rare environmental moments.

## Lore
Ask:
- “Tell me your story.”
- “Where did you come from?”
- “Why are you a blue ball?”
- “Why don't you have a face?”
- “Why don't you have arms?”
- “What's your earliest memory?”

## Rare scene engine
Scenes are intentionally uncommon and suppressed while the user is busy or the tab is hidden.

- Autumn: a leaf blows across Bluey's world and he dodges it.
- Halloween: Bluey can freeze at a mysterious passing moment and ask if you saw it too.
- Christmas: occasional snow; Bluey may wonder if something landed on him.
- July 4: Bluey wears a red-and-white visor (Bluey supplies the blue) and sparkler effects write HAPPY 4TH.
- July 7: Bluey's birthday gets a special party reaction and celebration.
- Existing holiday wardrobe remains.

## Design principle
The surprise is the feature. These events should never become constant background animation.

## Protected
The stable Chrome/Firefox microphone and transcription pipeline is unchanged.

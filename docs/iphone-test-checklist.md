# Bluey iPhone test checklist

About 15 minutes on an iPhone with Safari. Open https://bluey-ai-friend.vercel.app/ and go top to bottom.
Note anything that looks off with a screenshot (side button + volume up) and the step number.

Before you start: turn the ringer on (switch on the side) and the volume up, so you can hear Bluey.

## 1. First look (1 min)
1. The page loads with the blue orb and a greeting under it. Nothing overlaps the notch or the bottom bar.
2. The round blue **+** sits at the left of the message box, and **Send** at the right.
3. Tap the message box: the keyboard opens and the message box stays visible above it.

## 2. Chat and formatting (2 min)
4. Type `hi` and send. Bluey answers, and 2 or 3 blue-ball buttons appear under the reply.
5. Tap one of those buttons. It sends that message, the buttons disappear, and Bluey answers.
6. Send `Give me a packing list for a weekend camping trip, grouped by category.` The answer shows real bullet points and bold headings (no `**` or `#` symbols).
7. Tap **Copy** under that answer, then paste it into Notes. It pastes cleanly.

## 3. Voice (2 min)
8. Bluey speaks his reply out loud. (If not: tap **▶ Play again**.)
9. Open **+**: switch **Voice** off. The + shows a small 🔇 badge. Switch it back on.
10. In **+**, turn **Slower voice** on, then tap **▶ Play again** on a reply: it should sound slower and calmer.
11. Tap Bluey himself (the blue orb) to talk, and say "What's your favorite food?" Safari asks for the microphone once; allow it. Bluey should hear you (and say blueberries).

## 4. The + menu (3 min)
12. Tap **+**. You see: Add photos & files, Take a photo, Check a message for scams, Add Bluey to your home screen, Sound, Easier to use. Tap outside to close it.
13. **Take a photo**: the camera opens. Take a picture of anything and send `What is this?` Bluey describes it.
14. **Add photos & files**: pick a PDF or Word file from Files (an old résumé works). A chip shows its name and pages. Ask `What's the oldest job on it?`
15. **Easier to use**: tap **A++**. The chat text gets much bigger and nothing runs off the right edge. Tap **A** to go back.
16. **Simpler words**: turn it on and ask `What is a VPN?` The answer uses short, everyday words.

## 5. Scam check (2 min)
17. In **+**, tap **Check a message for scams**. The message box fills with "Is this a scam?".
18. Paste a real spam text if you have one (or type: `USPS: your package is on hold, pay $1.99 at usps-help-redelivery.com`) and send. A red **Looks like a scam** banner appears above the answer.
19. Try a screenshot instead: screenshot a spam text, add it with **+ > Add photos & files**, and send `Is this a scam?`

## 6. Home screen (2 min)
20. In **+**, tap **Add Bluey to your home screen**. A guide opens with pictures of each button: tap **⋯** next to the address bar, tap **Share**, scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, keep **Open as Web App** on, and tap **Add**. (On a second visit, Bluey also offers this with a "Make Bluey an app on your iPhone" card.)
21. Follow the steps. The icon on your Home Screen is a round blue ball named Bluey.
22. Open Bluey from the icon: it opens full screen, without Safari's address bar. The home-screen item is gone from the + menu.
23. Turn on Airplane Mode and open Bluey from the icon again. It opens, and the status line says "No signal right now." Turn Airplane Mode off.

## 7. Wrap-up (1 min)
24. Tap **New chat** and confirm. The chat clears.
25. Rotate the phone sideways and back. Nothing breaks.

Send back: the step numbers that failed, a screenshot of each, your iPhone model, and your iOS version (Settings > General > About).
